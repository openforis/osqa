{% blocktrans %}Programs read public questions, answers, tags and members as JSON without signing in. To post, or to see what only a member may see, a member creates a personal access token on their account and sends it in the Authorization header. A token can only do what its member may do on the site, with the same reputation rules and limits.{% endblocktrans %}
